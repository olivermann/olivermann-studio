In 2024, UBC's Office of Community Engagement engaged more than 1,600 faculty, staff, students and community partners through funding, programs and events. This report shares what that work looks like in practice, through the eyes of the people who use and shape it. Engagement is deeply relational, so the figures sit alongside partner stories, testimonials, videos and podcast episodes.

- The Community-University Engagement Support Fund awarded over $700,000 to 30 partnerships, and the Partnership Recognition & Exploration Fund supported 31 more.
- UBC Connects at Robson Square welcomed 10,000 attendees to 40 events.
- Eight Partnering in Research workshops drew more than 800 participants from Canada, the U.S. and Australia.
- The Community Partner Help Desk supported over 100 external partners, and the office produced UBC's first toolkit for honoraria payments.
- 24 stories were published, 15 of them featured on UBC's homepage.
